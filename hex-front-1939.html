Privacy Policy for Hex Front 1939

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Hex Front 1939 mobile game (the "App"), published on Google Play by Next_Era, handles information.

1. We collect no personal data

The App does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). It does not use the Advertising ID and does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Data stored on your device

The App saves your campaign progress, the battle in progress and your settings on your device only. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Optional WiFi duel

You can play against a second phone on the same WiFi network. During a duel the two phones exchange only the game moves and the state of the shared battle, and the game that is hosting shows a random label such as "Player 42" in the list of nearby games. No device name, device identifier or personal information is sent. This traffic stays on your local network: the App does not connect to the internet or to any server of ours. The network permissions the App requests (internet, network state, WiFi state, WiFi multicast) are needed only for this local duel.

4. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
